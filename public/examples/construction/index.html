Approach

We don't just build houses.We build places people stay.

A house is not finished when the trades leave. It is finished when a family has been through a summer in it and found that the doors still shut, the light still lands where it should, and nothing needs explaining.

What we build

Services

Four ways we work, all of them run the same way — a small number of builds at a time, with the people who own the company on site.

  1. 01

    Custom Homes

    A house built once, properly, for the people who will live in it — from an empty block or a knock-down, with the architect at the table from the start.

  2. 02

    Major Renovations

    Reworking a house you already love without pretending the existing building isn't there. Structure opened up, services replaced, the good bones kept.

  3. 03

    Extensions

    Adding the room the house has always been short of, joined so the seam doesn't show — in plan, in the roofline, and in the way the light lands.

  4. 04

    Design & Construct

    One team from first sketch to handover. Fewer gaps between drawing and building, and one person accountable for both.

How it runs

Process

01020304

Four stages, in order. You will know which one you are in, and what has to happen before the next.

  1. 01

    Conversation

    We walk the site and talk through what you want the house to do. Before drawings, before numbers — the brief is the part everything else is built on.

  2. 02

    Design

    We work alongside your architect, or bring one in. Buildability, materials and cost are discussed while the design can still absorb the answer.

  3. 03

    Build

    One site manager, one point of contact, and a schedule you can read. You will know what is happening this week and what is happening next.

  4. 04

    Handover

    The house is finished before it is handed over — not handed over with a list. We stay available long after the keys change hands.

Craft

The parts nobody photographs

What a house looks like on handover day is the easy part. What it looks like in ten years is decided in the details nobody thinks to take a photograph of.

Working closely with homeowners

Most of our clients are living through the build, or moving back into it. That changes how you run a site, and how often you pick up the phone.

Communication

No surprises at the end of the month. Decisions are put to you early, with the trade-offs attached, while they are still cheap to make.

Considered materials

Materials chosen for how they will look in ten years, not on handover day. Timber that greys evenly, concrete poured for the face it will show.

Attention to detail

Shadow lines that hold, doors that sit flush, tiles that run out whole at the edges. The parts nobody photographs are the parts that date a house.

Craftsmanship

A small number of builds at a time, run by the people who own the company. That is the only way we know to keep the standard where it is.

01 — The Studio

A room builtfor one thing:getting stronger.

No mirrors-and-neon fit-out, no circuit of machines to wander between. Platforms, bars, a turf lane and coaches who write the session before you walk in — five minutes from the beach at Mona Vale.

02 — Training

What wecoach here.

Four disciplines, one floor. Most members move between them across a week — the programming assumes you will.

  • 01

    Strength

    Barbell work on real platforms. Squat, press, hinge and pull, coached to a standard and loaded against a plan you can actually see.

    Platforms · Barbells · Programming

  • 02

    Conditioning

    Bikes, sleds, ropes and carries. Intervals written to a heart rate and a purpose rather than to the point of exhaustion.

    Intervals · Engine · Recovery

  • 03

    Athletic Performance

    Speed, change of direction and landing mechanics for people who still play something on the weekend and intend to keep doing it.

    Speed · Agility · Return to sport

  • 04

    Personal Training

    One to one, one hour, one focus. Programming, video review and the plain accountability of a slot with your name on it.

    1:1 · Assessment · Video review

03 — Method

Four steps,in order.

The sequence a new member moves through, and the one every block returns to afterwards.

01 / 0402 / 0403 / 0404 / 04
BuildMoveConditionPerform

Base strength. Positions first, then load that respects the position — squat, hinge, press, pull, carried through a block you can see the end of.

Range and control. The joint mechanics that let heavier work land safely, drilled often enough that they stop being a warm-up and start being how you move.

An engine built on purpose. Intervals, carries and steady work in a ratio that suits what you're training for, not whatever hurt the most last week.

The whole thing put back together and expressed somewhere real — on the field, in the surf, up the hill behind the studio.

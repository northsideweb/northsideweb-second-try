The standard

Anyone can wash
a car. Fewer
finish one.

Pristine Finish is a car cleaning service on Sydney's Northern Beaches, built on a simple idea — that the difference between clean and finished is entirely in the details other people skip.

Door shuts. Window rubbers. The seam where the seat meets the console. The inside face of the wheel. None of it is visible in a quick once-over, and all of it is the reason a car either looks washed or looks properly cared for.

Every clean is done by hand, with pH-neutral products and clean microfibre — because the fastest way to ruin good paint is a dirty mitt and a rushed job.

Why Pristine Finish

Care is a
method.

  • 01

    Done by hand

    No automated brushes, no recycled water, no swirl marks pressed into your clear coat by a machine that cleaned forty cars before yours.

  • 02

    Clean products, clean cloth

    pH-neutral chemistry and fresh microfibre on every car. The two-bucket method exists for a reason — grit that goes back on the paint is how paint gets ruined.

  • 03

    The parts nobody checks

    Door shuts, sills, fuel flap, window rubbers, the inside barrel of the wheel. It is the difference between a car that looks washed and one that looks finished.

  • 04

    One car at a time

    Your booking is not a slot in a queue. The work takes as long as the car needs, which is the only way any of the above is actually possible.

The Restaurant

One room, one fire, one view.

Everything happens in sight of everything else — that is the whole design of the place.

01

Dining Room

Forty seats, low light, and windows that stay open until the wind turns.

02

Kitchen

Open to the room. The grill runs on ironbark and nothing else.

03

Bar

Eight stools, walk-in only. Snacks until close.

04

Coastal Setting

The headland is a two-minute walk. Most of the menu comes from it.

Philosophy

Simple ingredients.Handled properly.

There is no long technique here and no foam. Fish that came in this morning, vegetables from up the coast, a fire that has been going since two o'clock, and salt. What we mostly do is decide when to stop.
